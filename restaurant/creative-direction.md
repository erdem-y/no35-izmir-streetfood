# No. 35 Original İzmir Streetfood — Creative Direction

## Brand
No.35 Original İzmir Streetfood

## Style
Modern premium streetfood + authentic İzmir/Aegean character.

## Atmosphere
İzmir, Çeşme, Alaçatı, Mediterranean summer evenings, warm light, streetfood, charcoal grill.

## Primary visual products
- Kumru
- Kokoreç
- Midye Dolma

## Website language
German.

## Primary goal
Present No.35 as a strong, professional restaurant brand and encourage Google reviews and physical visits. Online ordering is not the primary goal.

## Visual rules

- The supplied food photos are reference material.
- Do not use random Instagram images.
- Do not invent restaurant history.
- Do not invent products, prices or business information.
- AI-generated imagery will be created later through fal.ai.
